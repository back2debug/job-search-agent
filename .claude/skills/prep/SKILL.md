Prep me for this round. Interviewer: $ARGUMENTS

Read the company hub, `stories.md`, `learnings.md`, and the tailored CV first.

1. Research the interviewer — public professional background only. What they've built,
   how long they've been there, what they likely care about, what they'll probe given
   their role.
2. Predict 8–10 questions for this specific person, not generic ones. For each, name the
   story or angle from my bank that answers it, and how to open.
3. Flag any gap where I have no good story, and tell me how you'd handle it honestly.
4. Five questions for me to ask them — ones that show I've done the work and that surface
   something I actually want to know.
5. Pull the open items from `learnings.md` and give me two or three specific things to
   watch in this conversation.

Write it to `companies/[company]/[company]-round-N.md`. Reference the hub, don't restate it.
