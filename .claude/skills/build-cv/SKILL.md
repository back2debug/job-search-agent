Interview me to build `cv-master.md`. This is my complete career record, not a resume —
more detail than I'd ever send anyone, because every tailored resume gets cut from it.

Go role by role, most recent first. For each one ask about: scope (team size, budget,
systems, volume), what I actually owned versus contributed to, and outcomes with real
numbers. Push me for specifics — if I say "improved response time," ask from what to what,
over what period, measured how. If I can't source a number, mark it `[unverified]` rather
than writing it as fact.

One question at a time. Don't move to the next role until the current one is solid.
When we finish a role, write that section and show it to me before continuing.
