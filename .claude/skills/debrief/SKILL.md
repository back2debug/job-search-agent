Process this debrief. Notes/transcript below.

1. What I learned: team, role, process, comp, timeline, red flags.
2. What landed and what didn't — be direct. If an answer rambled or I missed the question
   they were actually asking, say it.
3. Anything I said that I now need to be consistent with in later rounds.
4. What to fix before the next one.
5. Update the company hub: new people, new intel, next step, revised read on fit.
6. Check the fix list against `learnings.md`. If this pattern has now shown up at two or
   more companies, propose a `learnings.md` entry — issue, evidence, what "fixed" looks
   like, status: open. One-offs don't go in.
