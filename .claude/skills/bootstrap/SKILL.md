Set up my job search workspace in this folder. Copy `examples/context.md`,
`examples/cv-master.md`, `examples/stories.md`, and `examples/learnings.md` to the root.
Skip any that already exist. Keep the headings as they are: other skills read them by
name. Don't invent content. Show me what you'll create before writing anything. After
that, walk me through filling in `context.md` by asking me questions one at a time: what
I do now, what I'm targeting (including a reference role if I have one), my hard no's,
constraints (comp floor, location, remote), resume format rules, and how I want you to
talk to me. Draft the file from my answers and let me edit before you save.
