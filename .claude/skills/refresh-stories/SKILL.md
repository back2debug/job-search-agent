Audit my story bank against the roles I'm actually interviewing for.

Map each story in `stories.md` to the competencies it covers. Then, from the hubs and round
files, work out what's actually being asked. Where's the gap?

Suggest experiences from `cv-master.md` that could fill it, and flag any story that's now
overused across companies or that keeps landing flat according to `learnings.md`.
Then interview me to build one or two new ones.
