Help me build `stories.md`. I want 5–8 interview stories, though we'll start with 3.

For each: Situation (2 sentences max), what I did (the specific decisions, not the team's),
Outcome with a number if one exists, and What this signals — the competency an interviewer
would actually be scoring.

Pull candidates from `cv-master.md` and suggest which experiences would make the strongest
stories, then interview me on the ones I pick. Tell me if a story is weak: no real conflict,
no decision I owned, or an outcome I can't defend.

Cross-check against `cv-master.md` when done. Flag any metric that contradicts, or any
achievement claimed in two stories.
